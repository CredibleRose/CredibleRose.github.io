20-second Ramadan commercial of 19 short shots on a packed modern Tashkent mahalla square at blue hour.

STYLE: maximal blockbuster energy — low heroic angles, fast circling dolly moves, sweeping crane shots, big lens flares, teal-and-orange grade, slow-motion details of bottles and caps, constant dynamic camera motion.

NATURAL BEHAVIOUR: people behave naturally and never pose or look into the lens; everyone is busy with their own thing and talks to each other. The boys play in a loose circle and sing to each other and to the people around, not to the camera. On the square someone is always giving, receiving or drinking a Pepsi, or hugging someone. MOOD: warm, kind and joyful, like the night the whole street comes out together to celebrate New Year. The party keeps growing shot by shot; every street frame is layered — passers-by crossing close in front of the lens, people congratulating and hugging behind, someone stepping out of a house far back. Many women and girls wear neat modern headscarves; hugs only between men or between women.

RULES: only the five boys sing the lyrics from @audio1; everyone else just talks. Hugs only between men or between women; men and women never hug or touch. Nobody clinks bottles. The boys mostly play the yellow cap doiras held up at shoulder height, struck with their fingers, jingles shaking; the tall Pepsi-can drums are played standing; no drum is ever held between the knees.

HARD CUTS: exactly 19 shots, cutting only at the marked times on the beat of @audio1; each shot a new angle. No dissolves.
