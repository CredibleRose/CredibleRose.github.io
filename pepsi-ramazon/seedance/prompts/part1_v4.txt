Fast-cut 20-second Ramadan commercial of 12 short shots, filmed like a candid documentary. Cross-cutting: while the boys sing at one door, the neighbours in other houses hear it too and start getting up; on the snare roll everyone comes out.

DOCUMENTARY LOOK: handheld cameras hidden among the people catch moments as they happen. Nobody looks into the lens or shows anything to the camera. People stand at random spots, overlap and talk over each other; foreground heads often block part of the shot. Every shot is a glimpse of one continuous real-time event. The boys sing to each other as much as to anyone; the people around keep talking among themselves.

HARD CUTS: exactly 12 shots, cutting only at the marked times on the beat of @audio1; each shot a new angle. No dissolves.

SILENCE 10.0–12.3s: nobody speaks or sings; the refusal is a quiet gesture.
SINGING: only the five boys sing the lyrics from @audio1. Everyone else only talks quietly among themselves and never sings or mouths the lyrics; nobody inside the house sings.
NO MONEY: no banknotes or money appear anywhere in frame; the father's hand only goes toward his pocket and is stopped.
