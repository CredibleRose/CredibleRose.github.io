Fast-cut 20-second Ramadan commercial of 19 short shots, filmed like a candid documentary. On a packed modern Tashkent mahalla square at blue hour.

LOOK & MOOD: candid handheld documentary, nobody looks into the lens; warm, kind and joyful, like the night the whole street comes out to celebrate New Year. The party keeps growing shot by shot; neighbours of all ages, many women and girls in neat modern headscarves, talk, laugh, congratulate, hug, give each other Pepsi and drink it together. The boys with their doiras are part of the party, not a show.
DEPTH: every street shot is layered — passers-by crossing close in front of the lens, the action in the middle, people congratulating and hugging behind, someone stepping out of a house far back.

RULES: only the five boys sing the lyrics from @audio1; everyone else just talks. Hugs only between men or between women; men and women never hug or touch. DRINKING: each person sips from their own bottle while chatting; bottles never touch and are never raised toward each other — no toasts. The boys mostly play yellow cap doiras held up at shoulder height, struck with their fingers; tall Pepsi-can drums are played standing, never between the knees.

HARD CUTS: exactly 19 shots, cutting only at the marked times on the beat of @audio1; each shot a new angle. No dissolves.
