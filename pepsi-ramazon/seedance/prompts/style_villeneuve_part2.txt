20-second Ramadan commercial of 10 shots on a packed modern Tashkent mahalla square at blue hour: Pepsi codes, a Paynet win, the Kia prize and the whole street celebrating together.

STYLE: monumental minimalism — vast symmetrical wide shots where people are small against the houses and sky, slow measured camera moves, long lenses, silhouettes in haze, muted desaturated palette with a strong warm-against-cool contrast, quiet weight before every hit.

CUTS: exactly 10 shots, cutting only at the marked times on the beat of @audio1. Every shot is staged and moved in the camera language above.

RULES: only the five boys sing the lyrics from @audio1; everyone else just talks; nobody looks into the lens or poses. Hugs only between men or between women; men and women never hug or touch. Each person drinks from their own bottle; bottles never touch, no toasts. The boys play yellow cap doiras held up at shoulder height, struck with their fingers; tall Pepsi-can drums are played standing, never between the knees; no drumsticks. Dozens of neighbours, many women and girls in neat modern headscarves; the crowd grows from shot to shot, warm and joyful like a New Year night on the street.
