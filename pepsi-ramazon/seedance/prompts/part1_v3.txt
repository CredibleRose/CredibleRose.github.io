Fast-cut 20-second Ramadan commercial of 14 short shots, filmed like a candid documentary. Cut on matching shapes and rhythm: spoon, the grandfather's palm, the round non, the round doira in the doorway, the flipped cap, the drumhead on the drop.

DOCUMENTARY LOOK: handheld cameras hidden among the people catch moments as they happen. Nobody looks into the lens or shows anything to the camera. People stand at random spots, overlap and talk over each other; foreground heads often block part of the shot. Every shot is a glimpse of one continuous real-time event. The boys sing to each other as much as to anyone; the people around keep talking among themselves.

HARD CUTS: exactly 14 shots, cutting only at the marked times on the beat of @audio1; each shot a new angle. No dissolves.

SILENCE 10.0–12.3s: nobody speaks or sings; the refusal is gesture and laughter. Nobody inside the house ever sings — the chanting comes from the boys outside.
