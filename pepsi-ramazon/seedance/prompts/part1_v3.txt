Fast-cut 20-second Ramadan commercial of 12 short shots, filmed like a candid documentary. Seen from a child's eye level: the kids hear the song first, get to the door before their father and are first out into the street.

DOCUMENTARY LOOK: handheld cameras hidden among the people catch moments as they happen. Nobody looks into the lens or shows anything to the camera. People stand at random spots, overlap and talk over each other; foreground heads often block part of the shot. Every shot is a glimpse of one continuous real-time event. The boys sing to each other as much as to anyone; the people around keep talking among themselves.

HARD CUTS: exactly 12 shots, cutting only at the marked times on the beat of @audio1; each shot a new angle. No dissolves.
