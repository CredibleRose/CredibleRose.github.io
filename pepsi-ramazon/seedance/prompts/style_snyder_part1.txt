20-second Ramadan commercial of 12 short shots. An ordinary evening caught as it happens: a family iftar, a song at the door, the boys gently stop the father's hand on its way to his pocket, the street comes out.

STYLE: stylised speed ramps synced to the drum hits — fast, then slow motion, then fast again — high-contrast desaturated grade with crushed blacks, strong rim light and silhouettes, dust and light particles floating in the air, painterly tableau compositions with shallow depth of field.

NATURAL BEHAVIOUR: people behave naturally and never pose or look into the lens; everyone is busy with their own thing and talks to each other. The boys play in a loose circle and sing to each other and to the people around, not to the camera. On the square someone is always giving, receiving or drinking a Pepsi, or hugging someone.

HARD CUTS: exactly 12 shots, cutting only at the marked times on the beat of @audio1; each shot a new angle. No dissolves.

SILENCE 10.0–12.3s: nobody speaks or sings; the refusal is a quiet gesture.
SINGING: only the five boys sing the lyrics from @audio1. Everyone else only talks quietly among themselves and never sings or mouths the lyrics; nobody inside the house sings.
NO MONEY: no banknotes or money appear anywhere in frame; the father's hand only goes toward his pocket and is stopped.
