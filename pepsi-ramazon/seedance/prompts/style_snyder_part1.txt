20-second Ramadan commercial of 13 short shots. An ordinary evening caught as it happens: a family iftar, a song at the door, the boys gently stop the father's hand on its way to his pocket, the street comes out.

STYLE: stylised speed ramps synced to the drum hits — fast, then slow motion, then fast again — high-contrast desaturated grade with crushed blacks, strong rim light and silhouettes, dust and light particles floating in the air, painterly tableau compositions with shallow depth of field.

NATURAL BEHAVIOUR: people behave naturally and never pose or look into the lens; everyone is busy with their own thing and talks to each other. The boys play in a loose circle and sing to each other and to the people around, not to the camera. On the square someone is always giving, receiving or drinking a Pepsi, or hugging someone. MOOD: warm, kind and joyful, like the night the whole street comes out together to celebrate New Year. The party keeps growing shot by shot; every street frame is layered — passers-by crossing close in front of the lens, people congratulating and hugging behind, someone stepping out of a house far back. Many women and girls wear neat modern headscarves; hugs only between men or between women.

HARD CUTS: exactly 13 shots, cutting only at the marked times on the beat of @audio1; each shot a new angle. No dissolves.

SILENCE 10.0–12.3s: nobody speaks or sings; the refusal is a quiet gesture.
RULES: only the five boys sing the lyrics from @audio1; everyone else just talks, nobody inside the house sings. No money anywhere in frame; the father's hand only moves toward his pocket and is stopped. The boys hold their yellow cap doiras up at shoulder height and strike them with their fingers; no drum between the knees. No hugging or touching between men and women. DRINKING: each person sips from their own bottle while chatting; bottles never touch and are never raised toward each other — no toasts.
