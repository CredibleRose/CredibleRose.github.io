Fast-cut 20-second Ramadan commercial of 17 short shots, filmed like a candid documentary: a family iftar in a modern Tashkent home, five young men at the door who refuse money and ask for Pepsi, then the mahalla wakes up on the drum drop.

DOCUMENTARY LOOK: handheld cameras hidden among the people catch moments as they happen. Nobody looks into the lens or shows anything to the camera. People stand at random spots, overlap and talk over each other; foreground heads often block part of the shot. Every shot is a glimpse of one continuous real-time event.

HARD CUTS: exactly 17 shots, cutting only at the marked times on the beat of @audio1; each shot a new angle. No dissolves.

SILENCE 10.0–12.3s: nobody speaks or sings; the refusal is gesture and laughter. Nobody inside the house ever sings — the chanting comes from the boys outside.
