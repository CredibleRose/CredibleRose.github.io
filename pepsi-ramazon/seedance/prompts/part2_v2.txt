Fast-cut 20-second Ramadan commercial of 19 short shots, filmed like a candid documentary. On a packed mahalla square at blue hour, the camera travels with one Pepsi bottle passed from hand to hand; every cut lands on a new pair of hands.

DOCUMENTARY LOOK: handheld cameras hidden in the crowd catch moments as they happen. Nobody looks into the lens or shows a phone, cap or bottle to the camera. Dozens of people of all ages at random spots, each busy with their own thing; foreground heads often block part of the shot. The boys play in a loose circle inside the crowd and sing to each other and to the neighbours next to them — never toward the camera, never like a stage show. Around them people keep talking to each other: greeting, joking, chatting over the music. In every street shot someone is giving, receiving or drinking a Pepsi, or hugging someone.
