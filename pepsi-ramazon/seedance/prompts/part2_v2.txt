Fast-cut 20-second Ramadan commercial of 19 short shots, filmed like a candid documentary. On a packed mahalla square at blue hour, the camera travels with one Pepsi bottle passed from hand to hand; every cut lands on a new pair of hands.

LOOK & MOOD: candid handheld documentary, nobody looks into the lens. Warm, kind and joyful, like the night the whole street comes out together to celebrate New Year. The party keeps growing — every shot has more people than the last; neighbours of all ages, many women and girls in neat modern headscarves, talk, laugh, congratulate each other, hug, give and drink Pepsi. The boys with their doiras are part of the party, singing to each other and to people nearby, never a show.
DEPTH: every street shot is layered — passers-by cross close in front of the lens, the main action in the middle, neighbours congratulating behind, people hugging further back, someone stepping out of a house far back.

RULES: only the five boys sing the lyrics from @audio1; everyone else just talks. Hugs only between men or between women; men and women never hug or touch. Nobody clinks bottles. The boys mostly play the yellow cap doiras held up at shoulder height, struck with their fingers, jingles shaking; the tall Pepsi-can drums are played standing; no drum is ever held between the knees.

HARD CUTS: exactly 19 shots, cutting only at the marked times on the beat of @audio1; each shot a new angle. No dissolves.
